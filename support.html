Tuck-In Tales

Help

Personalized, animated bedtime stories where your kid is the hero.

How do I add my child?

Tap + Add a kid at the top. Enter their name and pick their look, pet, best friend and grown-up. Every story changes to star them.

How do I unlock more stories?

The app comes with 25 stories. Tap any story with a lock, or open Story packs in the library. Each pack adds 10 stories for a one-time $1.

I got a new phone. How do I get my packs back?

Open Parents, answer the grown-ups question, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple or Google account you bought them with.

The story isn't reading out loud

Turn the volume up. On iPhone, read-aloud can follow the silent switch, so make sure it's off.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone and iPad) or through your Google Play order history (Android).

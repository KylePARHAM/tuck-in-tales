Privacy Policy

Tuck-In Tales · Effective November 8, 2026

Tuck-In Tales is a bedtime-story app for children, set up by their parents. It is published by Palm Beach Pre Purchase Inspections LLC ("we"). We built it so that nothing about your child ever leaves your device. This policy explains what that means.

The short version

  • We don't collect any personal information about you or your child.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party software development kits (SDKs).
  • Kid profiles stay on your phone or tablet.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Information you enter in the app

To personalize stories, a parent can enter a child's first name, age, pronouns, the look of their cartoon character (skin tone, hair, favorite color), and the names of a pet, best friend and grown-up. This information is stored only on the device, inside the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see it.

Read-aloud

Stories are read aloud with your device's built-in voice. The app doesn't record audio and doesn't use the microphone.

Purchases

Story packs are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment under their own privacy policies. We never receive your name, email or payment details. The app only learns which story packs are unlocked, so it can open them and restore them.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children with their parents. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app doesn't let children contact anyone, share anything, or see ads. Purchases and the parent area are behind a grown-ups-only question.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Deleting information

To delete a child's profile, open it in the app and tap Delete. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. Purchases stay with your Apple or Google account and can be restored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.

Questions

Parents ask

What ages is it for?

Ages 3 to 8. Little ones love seeing themselves; older kids read along.

Can I add more than one child?

Yes. Add each child and switch between them. Every story changes to star whoever is picked.

Does it read the story out loud?

Yes. Tap "Read to me" and it reads each page with your device's voice.

Is my child's information private?

Yes. Names and looks are stored only on your device. We never see them. Read the privacy policy.

Will my packs carry over to a new phone?

Yes. Open Parents and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple or Google account.
